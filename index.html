The problem isn't complexity. It's coordination.

A typical 150-question vendor security questionnaire contains roughly 40 unique concepts. Your organization almost certainly has documented answers to 80–90% of them — scattered across SharePoint, Google Drive, email attachments, and a compliance folder no one's touched since 2023.

The six-week timeline exists because finding, matching, routing, reviewing, and packaging those answers is pure coordination work. ClearPath eliminates the coordination overhead and leaves only the judgment calls that actually require human expertise.

Your compliance team stops being a relay race. They start being reviewers.
